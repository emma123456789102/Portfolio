The philosphy

Colleena Shakti's work is an intersection of devotion, tradition, and imaginative innovation. Her teaching methodology blends Indian classical dance, yoga, contemporary movement, somatic practice, and the philosophical traditions that inform them.
The PRACTICE
Dive into various elements of dance and somatic movement. Colleena teaches students strong classical dance technique as well as theory, and transcendence through her deep interest in the philosophical traditions behind the ancient practices. Her art embodies devotion, and whether you are new to Odissi, yoga, or Fusion, or a seasoned dancer, Colleena's teaching helps students find true embodiment in movement.
